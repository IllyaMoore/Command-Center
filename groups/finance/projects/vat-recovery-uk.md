## VAT Invoice Checklist

Every invoice included in the claim MUST contain ALL of the following. If any element is missing, contact the supplier and ask them to reissue:

1. **Supplier's name and address**
2. **Supplier's VAT registration number**
3. **Invoice date**
4. **Invoice number** (unique sequential number)
5. **Description of goods/services**
6. **Net amount (excluding VAT)**
7. **VAT rate applied** (should be 20% for standard rate)
8. **VAT amount**
9. **Gross amount (including VAT)**
10. **Customer name** — ideally "The Borderlands Foundation" (Booking.com receipts addressed to the guest personally are NOT acceptable for VAT claims)

**Critical:** Booking.com receipts and confirmations explicitly state "you can't use it to claim VAT." All invoices must come directly from the property/supplier.
